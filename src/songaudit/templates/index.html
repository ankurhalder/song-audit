SongAudit

Every song, audited in one pass.

Drop a track or paste a direct audio link. You get loudness, true peak, clipping, stereo phase, tonal balance, tempo, key, sections and a weighted scorecard with prioritized fixes. No microphone, no virtual cable, no plugins.

Audio stays on this machine.
Suno share pages are app-rendered and do not expose an audio file; download the track from Suno first, then drop the file here.

Objective mastering

BS.1770 loudness, 4x true peak, clipping runs, DC offset, LRA and noise floor.

Musical structure

Tempo, key, tuning and chroma-based section boundaries with per-section energy.

Actionable grade

Weighted scorecard, letter grade and a ranked fix list - not just pretty charts.

Open source - MIT. Runs fully locally; nothing leaves the machine.
