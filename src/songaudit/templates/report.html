How to read this

Loudness follows ITU-R BS.1770-4 (LUFS) with 4x oversampled true-peak detection. Tonal balance compares octave-band energy with the track's own fitted tilt, so it measures balance rather than a fixed "correct" curve. Key and tempo use librosa's chroma and beat trackers; sections come from agglomerative clustering of chroma and are heuristic.

This audit is not a plagiarism check, not an AI-generation detector, and not a substitute for a mastering engineer who knows the genre. Targets are streaming-oriented (-14 LUFS, -1 dBTP). Scores describe measured signals, not taste.
